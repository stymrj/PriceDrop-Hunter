Live test

Check a real price in about five minutes.

The sample above is illustrative. This is how to run a real cross-store comparison for any keyword:

  1. Pick one keyword — a product sold on several stores, e.g. boAt Airdopes 141 for electronics or Aashirvaad Atta 10 kg for groceries (Myntra and Croma do not stock groceries, so compare those two on electronics or fashion instead).
  2. Install each store's Hunter from its install page (bookmarklet or userscript), then open the store and set your delivery pincode or address.
  3. On each store, open Hunter, press Scan, and search your keyword in the search box (commas mean "or" on JioMart: airdopes, earbuds).
  4. Export CSV from each store (menu → Export → CSV) and paste each file here, one store at a time — or just note the best price per store and compare by hand.
  5. Compare like for like: same variant and pack size, same pincode, in-stock only. Prices differ by delivery address, so a comparison only means something for one pincode at one time.
  6. Sanity-check the winner: open the product on the winning store and confirm the price and delivery slot before buying — a scanned price can change between scan and checkout.
