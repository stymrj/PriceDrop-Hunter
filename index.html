Install once

One bookmark for every store.

1

Add the bookmark

Drag the yellow PriceDrop Hunter button at the top to your bookmarks bar. On a phone, tap Copy for mobile, bookmark any page, and paste the code as its address.

2

Open a store

Go to Flipkart Minutes, Blinkit, Amazon Now, Swiggy Instamart or BigBasket and set your delivery address.

3

Click it

The bookmark sees which store you are on and opens that store's PriceDrop Hunter. Always the latest version.

Universal bookmark code

A few hundred characters, so it fits on any phone. Prefer something that works offline? Each store page also has a full, self-contained bookmarklet and a userscript.

Telegram alerts

Deals on your phone, two ways.

In the browser

Alerts while you shop

Connect your own Telegram bot in Hunter's sidebar. After each scan it sends new deals, price drops and cheaper saved items. Turn on auto-scan to check every 10 to 60 minutes while the tab is open.

On GitHub Actions

Alerts around the clock

Run the bot on GitHub Actions, free. Every 30 minutes it scans and sends new deals, price drops and restocks, plus a morning catalogue. Available for Flipkart Minutes, Blinkit, Amazon Now and BigBasket.

FAQ

Good questions.

Is it free?

Yes. Everything runs in your browser or on your own free GitHub account. There is no server and no subscription.

Does it see my data?

No. Hunter runs inside your own browser tab with your own store session. Saved items and price history stay in your browser.

Why are the deals different from my friend's?

Quick-commerce prices and stock depend on the dark store near your delivery address, so everyone sees their own local deals.

Is it official?

No. PriceDrop Hunter is independent and not affiliated with any of these stores. It only reads what your account can already see.

Nothing happens on my phone.

Make sure you pasted the whole code as the bookmark's address, then open the store and pick the bookmark from the address-bar suggestions instead of the bookmarks menu.

Where does the data come from?

Live from the store, inside your own logged-in tab, using your delivery address. Nothing is stored on any server.
